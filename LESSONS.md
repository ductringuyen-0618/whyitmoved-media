## Starting defaults (from launch review, not yet tested by data)
- Frame 1 must show the move and the question. No logo intro, no empty first second.
- Lead with the surprise ("fell on good news", "both sides of a deal crashed"), not the company profile.
- One "why" beat per video (chain or scorecard) that a non-investor gets in one watch.
- Max 3–4 numbers on screen. Say the rest in plain words.
- Outro lesson is a reusable rule of thumb, never repeated within 2 weeks.
