# Why It Moved — lessons

Maintained by the weekly feedback run (Sundays). Every production run reads this file and follows it.
Style guidance only: it never overrides fact-checking, sourcing or no-advice rules.

## Current rules (from data)
All rules are EARLY SIGNAL (week 1, Nov 2-8, n=15 TikTok posts, 7-534 views each, confounded by posting time and account age). Style only; they never touch facts, sourcing, disclaimers or the no-advice policy.
1. Prefer one-story "surprise" videos on a household or big-cap name (capital raise, stock-paid deal, beat-but-fell) over rank cards. Evidence (n=3 vs n=4, TikTok views): MRVL 534, ORCL 319, PLTR 229 vs ranking/weekly 192, 157, 111 and preview 40. Max 1 ranking video per 7 days.
2. Daily videos target 35-40 s, not 30-32 s. Evidence (n=9 vs n=6): median TikTok views 157 for videos of 36 s or more vs 97 for 35 s or less (RIOT 31 s got 7, FN 31 s 122, SHOP 31 s 72). Still the 30 s minimum; this only says do not trim for trimming's sake.
3. Put the answer's first clause (the "because") by second 5. Evidence: YouTube avg view duration is 2-12 s on 31-40 s videos (n=7; ORCL 8 s, MRVL 9 s, SHOP 6 s, PLTR 6 s, LSCC 2 s), so most viewers leave in the first third. The one exception, the QCOM preview (12 s), had only 26 views.
4. End with a concrete either/or fork tied to this story, not "Did the market overreact?" or "Which surprised you most?". Evidence: MRVL "Good deal or overpaid?" drew 2 comments, the week's most; "Did the market overreact?" drew 0, 0, 1 on ORCL, PLTR, SHOP. Total TikTok comments in the week: 3 of 15 posts (n too small to rely on).

## Hold-offs (tested, no rule yet)
- The content reviewer's and Content Master's Hook scores did not predict views this week: H4 posts got 229 (PLTR), 72 (SHOP), 7 (RIOT); H3 posts got 143-192. Do not treat a score of 4-5 as evidence of performance until 30+ posts.
- Posting time cannot be judged yet: the three best posts went out 23:20-23:55 PT on launch day (account's first hours), so time, topic and format are confounded. See the report for the schedule note.

## Starting defaults (from launch review, not yet tested by data)
- Frame 1 must show the move and the question. No logo intro, no empty first second.
- Lead with the surprise ("fell on good news", "both sides of a deal crashed"), not the company profile.
- One "why" beat per video (chain or scorecard) that a non-investor gets in one watch.
- Max 3–4 numbers on screen. Say the rest in plain words.
- Outro lesson is a reusable rule of thumb, never repeated within 2 weeks.
