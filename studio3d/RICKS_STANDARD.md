# Reference standard: @RicksClips3000 (reverse-engineered from 3 top shorts, Nov 2026)
Source videos: VS1Z_Q-Klt8 (Ancient Jar, 31.8M), 7xS87iActkU (Perfect Railroads, 22.5M), 0m6G8pDtzNs (Japan job, 16.7M). Analysis is AI-generated from the video, so mm/hex/fps numbers are estimates.

## Architecture (same as our engine)
3D low-poly toon world + 2D billboard bean-head characters (flat, unlit, black outline 2-3px) facing camera. Complex props are 3D cel-shaded.
## Characters
Bean/capsule head (2-2.5 heads tall), dot eyes, arc brows, 1-frame mouth/eye swaps (no morphing), stick limbs, flat torso (vest/jacket). Limbs and walk cycles on TWOS (12 fps) inside smooth 30 fps camera. Anticipation 1-2 frames, strong squash/stretch on laugh/fear/rage, sweat beads, steam from ears, colour tint on anger.
## Camera
Changes every ~3 s. Mix: slow push-ins, 360 orbits around hero props, low-angle hero shots, high-angle wide scale shots, whip pans, dutch tilt, macro close-up with shallow DOF, zoom punches on every impact beat, screen shake synced to audio hits, handheld drift. Wide lens 18-28 mm for scale, 35 mm dialogue, 85 mm macro.
## Light / grade (per scene, never one global look)
Warm interior firelight + rim light on props; hard noon sun with ground shadows; sunset gradient + backlight flare; night = cobalt blue with ONE warm local source (torch/lantern); sickly green fluorescent for gloom; god-rays through windows; heat shimmer/red-purple tint for heat; fire glow on explosions. Soft vignette, 3-5% paper grain. Saturated palettes.
## Set storytelling
Readable text on props (signs, calendars, breaker labels, phone screens, name plates). Tiny crowd instances for scale. Cutaway/cross-section diagrams for the explanation beat.
## Captions
Bottom-centre third, heavy rounded sans, white fill + 4px black stroke, active word yellow, pop-scale 115%->100% in 3 frames per word.
## Audio
Narration fast and deadpan ~160-180 wpm. Music lo-fi/ragtime ~90-95 BPM, ducked -10 to -14 dB under voice. Dense SFX: a sound on every visible event (clink, clang, cha-ching, swivel squeak, ticking clock motif, breaker clack, sub-bass boom, crowd laugh), ambience beds (wind, crickets, city hum), silence before a reveal.
## Structure
Hook = in-media-res action + counter-intuitive claim in first 2 s. 60-66 s explainer story: setup, mocked/ignored, reversal, mechanism cutaway, payoff, final line that loops to the opening.
## Gaps in our engine vs this standard
captions (none), cut rate (ours ~5-8 s shots), camera moves (ours linear push), per-scene grade, local lights/god rays/vignette/grain, twos-stepped motion, impact zoom punches + shake, SFX density, hook action, loop ending.
